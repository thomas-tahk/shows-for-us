Shows For Us Plan

A public wiki for musicals, built on Wikidata and Wikipedia, with 15 flagship shows written up in depth and live "playing next" dates from Ticketmaster. Three milestones. Only Milestone 1 is broken into issues.

Project done-gate: I search a musical I love, read its history and who wrote it and starred in it, see where it's playing next, and click through to buy tickets.

Approve with these four calls

  1. Keep the React frontend; set the Express/Postgres backend aside. Show data is built into the site, so a static site is enough. One small Vercel function fetches Ticketmaster dates live, because Ticketmaster's terms forbid storing them. backend/ stays untouched in M1, and so do your uncommitted axios→fetch changes. I'll ask before deleting it later.
  2. Agents pick the 15 flagship shows; you swap any. They choose by Tony and Pulitzer wins plus whether the show is touring or running now. Each page is drafted from Wikipedia and Wikidata with sources cited, and you spot-check it at the end of M1.
  3. Hosted on Vercel's free tier. It never sleeps, and the frontend already has a Vercel config. No database, so there's nothing to pause.
  4. You do one thing: put your Ticketmaster key into Vercel. You already have it in backend/.env. Until it's there, pages show your hand-listed runs plus the official ticket site, and nothing breaks.

Milestones

NEXT

M1 · Flagship shows, end to end

Search, 15 in-depth show pages (history, creators, original cast, notable productions), live "playing next" and buy-ticket links. Live on a public URL.

Gate: the project done-gate, for any of the 15 flagship shows.

THEN

M2 · Every musical

About 6,000 musicals with a Wikipedia article get a lighter page: summary, creators, premiere, awards, and links out to IBDB, Playbill and BroadwayWorld. Search covers all of them. The data refreshes weekly.

Gate: I search an obscure musical that isn't a flagship, land on its page, and follow its IBDB link.

THEN

M3 · People

Pages for composers, lyricists, writers and performers: their shows and roles, linked both ways from show pages.

Gate: from a show page I click a composer, see their other shows, and open one.

LATER

Possible · Tracker layer, beyond musicals

Favorites and "near me" alerts from the 2025 plan, plus plays and opera. These are non-goals for now and get a Shape round before anything is built.
