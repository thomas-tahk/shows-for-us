# Milestone 2: Every musical

**Done-gate:** "I search an obscure musical that isn't a flagship, land on its page, and follow its IBDB link."

**Status:** passes in a browser against the real data, except the last click. The cloud can't open ibdb.com, so the IBDB page itself is unchecked. You haven't tried it yet.

- A fresh QA agent ran the gate 10 times: 5 obscure shows, each at phone size (390×844) and desktop size (1280×800). The 5 shows were Red, Hot and Blue, The Drowsy Chaperone, Florodora, The Little Show and Kiss of the Spider Woman. Each query had a typo or was cut short ("red hot and blu", "florodoro", "kiss of the spidr woman"). Every time, the right show came first in under 160 ms, and its page had the summary, creators and an IBDB link of the form `https://www.ibdb.com/broadway-show/<number>`.
- A show page loads about 420 KB, most of it the app itself, plus one ~50–100 KB data file. It never downloads the 1 MB search list.
- **The data is real.** It was pulled by the "Refresh data" GitHub Action ([run](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/shows-for-us/actions/runs/41840143245)), because the cloud can't reach Wikidata or Wikipedia:
  - 2,396 musicals, each with a Wikipedia summary.
  - 946 of them have an IBDB link.
  - The same run also pulled your 15 flagship shows live.
- Tests at the head: typecheck and lint are clean, `npm test` passes 233/233, Playwright passes 39/39 at both widths, and the build passes. GitHub CI is green on the branch.

**Scorecard so far:** [`docs/chief/scorecard.md`](../chief/scorecard.md). First-pass reviews 1/8, merged/planned 6/6 (stuck 0).

## How to try it

On your phone or work browser, signed in to Vercel: **https://shows-for-us-git-m2-every-musical-thomas-tahks-projects.vercel.app**

1. On the home page, type `red hot and blu` and tap **Red, Hot and Blue** (Cole Porter, 1936).
2. Read **From Wikipedia**, **Created by** and **Premiere**.
3. Tap **IBDB** under **Links**. It should open Red, Hot and Blue's IBDB page.
4. Try any musical you know that isn't one of the 15 flagships, misspelled on purpose.

Watch for:
- Shows that aren't musicals. About 1 in 10 still slip through: plays, a song, an album, a dance. More on this in decision 1.
- Flagship pages (e.g. Hamilton) now have their Commons photo and a "From Wikipedia" summary. M1 hid these until live data existed.

**Fixed along the way** (caught by reviewers before merge):
- The weekly refresh would have failed every run on a path bug. A branch-name clash would also have broken it after M1's first refresh.
- `/show/constructor` and similar names would have blanked the whole app.
- Search ranked Book of Mormon above The Robber Bridegroom for "robber", and Phantom above everything for "heart". Apostrophe and hyphen titles (Ain't Misbehavin', Spider-Man) were unfindable.
- Tests checked sample data, so the first live refresh would have turned the weekly job red forever.
- Wikidata errors would have been read as "no summary". Decade-precision dates would have shown as fake exact dates. Persons with only a "mul" name label would have lost their credits.
- My own wrong Wikidata ids: "ballet" was actually Münster. Caught by a reviewer. Specialists must now verify every id ([.claude/agents/shows-for-us-curator.md](../../.claude/agents/shows-for-us-curator.md)).

**Known, not fixed:**
- About 1 in 10 catalogue entries aren't musicals: plays, a song from Grease, an ABBA album, a Marathi film. Some operettas the text rule can't decide fall either way. The Threepenny Opera depends on its Wikidata class.
- 28 titles belong to two shows (two Aladdins, two Cinderellas). In search they look alike when neither has a year.
- No BroadwayWorld links. Wikidata has no BroadwayWorld ID for shows.
- No pictures on catalogue pages.
- One-letter typos in short words ("redd") find nothing. Pressing Back from a show clears the search box.
- The IBDB link opens in the same tab, like M1's flagship links.

**Stuck:** none. #15 closes when you merge (its real run passed).

## Decisions that need you (most important first)

1. **Musicals only, by this rule?** I dropped operas, operettas (including Gilbert & Sullivan and Lehár), oratorios and ballets. 2,396 shows are left, and about 1 in 10 are still off: plays, songs, films. Say if you want operettas back, or a stricter filter that costs some real musicals.
2. **The catalogue page layout:** From Wikipedia, Created by (Music, Lyrics, Book), Premiere, Awards, then Links. No "Playing next", because live dates stay flagship-only to protect the Ticketmaster quota.
3. **No BroadwayWorld links,** because no show ID exists to build them from. OK?
4. **Search wording:** "Featured shows" (empty search), "N shows found — showing the first 50", and "The full catalogue couldn't load, so only the featured shows are searchable."
5. **Flagship pages now show the Commons photo and the Wikipedia summary,** from the live data. Keep them?
6. **Show URLs come from Wikipedia titles.** A new same-named musical can rename one, e.g. `aladdin` becomes `aladdin-musical`. OK for now?
7. **M1's 6 decisions** in [`M1.md`](M1.md) are still open. Merging this also brings M1's live flagship data to main, so M1 step 3 is done.

Every other interpretation is in [`docs/questions/M2.md`](../questions/M2.md).
